# Edge AI Training Lab surface brief

- Mode: Operate. Audience: learners understanding neural network training.
- Job: Choose data, inspect model structure, train, and connect observed loss and decision boundary to the chosen settings.
- Scope: `static/apps/edge-ai-sandbox.html`. Preserve training, comparison, charts, logs, stop, export, and browser-only execution. Keep mobile and keyboard use sound. No sitewide Gatsby or plugin changes.
